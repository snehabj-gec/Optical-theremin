Testing and Experimental Results

1. Testing Setup

The Optical Theremin prototype was assembled and tested using a 9V power supply.

The testing process involved evaluating the pitch-control and volume-control sections independently, followed by testing the complete audio output.

2. Functional Testing

Test| Parameter Evaluated| Expected Behaviour
NE555 oscillator| Output frequency| Continuous square-wave generation
Pitch control| LDR resistance| Change in output frequency
Volume control| Audio output level| Variation in sound intensity
Audio amplifier| Speaker output| Audible sound reproduction
Complete system| Overall operation| Independent pitch and volume control

3. Observations

- The NE555 generated the audio-frequency square-wave signal.
- Variations in light intensity affected the pitch-control section.
- The second LDR provided volume control.
- The LM386 amplified the audio signal to drive the speaker.
- The complete prototype demonstrated optical control of pitch and volume.

4. Practical Challenges

During prototype development, the following issues were encountered:

- Initial high-frequency sound and intermittent audio output.
- Volume-control response required troubleshooting.
- Battery power consumption required attention.
- Hardware connections were checked and corrected during testing.

5. Results

The developed prototype successfully demonstrated light-dependent control of audio pitch and volume.

6. Limitations

- Performance depends on ambient light conditions.
- The frequency response depends on LDR characteristics.
- Quantitative measurements are required for detailed performance evaluation.

7. Future Improvements

- Improved frequency stability.
- Better optical sensitivity.
- Reduced power consumption.
- Improved PCB layout and hardware integration.
- Quantitative testing using an oscilloscope and frequency measurement equipment.
