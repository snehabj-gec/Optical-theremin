Project Images

This folder contains photographs documenting the development and implementation of the Optical Theremin project.

Contents

The images may include:

- Breadboard prototype
- Circuit testing and experimentation
- Component connections
- Dot PCB assembly
- Final assembled circuit
- Working demonstration

Purpose

These photographs provide visual documentation of the project's development, from initial prototyping to the final hardware implementation.

They also serve as evidence of the practical construction and testing of the Optical Theremin.
